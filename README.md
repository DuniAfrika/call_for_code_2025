# 📱 Linda: AI-Powered Safety Assistant for Informal Workers in Nairobi

**Linda** is an AI-powered safety assistant designed to improve Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) for informal sector workers in Nairobi’s Central Business District (CBD). From roadside welders to carpenters and masons, these workers face daily hazards without access to formal safety systems. Linda delivers real-time, personalized safety coaching—right through WhatsApp.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Over **60% of Nairobi’s workforce** operates informally—without regulatory protection, training, or employer-provided safety tools. Workers in these sectors are exposed to:

* Unregulated machinery and equipment
* Unstable work environments (e.g., scaffolding, weak buildings)
* Lack of personal protective equipment (PPE)
* High-risk tasks performed without formal guidance

These unsafe conditions contribute to **preventable injuries**, **loss of income**, and **public health strain**, limiting economic resilience and growth.

---

## 💡 The Solution: Linda

**Linda** is an AI-based assistant that embeds safety guidance into tools workers already use—**WhatsApp**—making safety education **accessible, conversational, and real-time**.

### ✅ Core Features

* 📷 **Photo-Based Hazard Detection**
  Workers send a photo of their work environment to Linda via WhatsApp.
  → **AI (Granite Vision)** analyzes the image and identifies hazards like missing PPE, unstable structures, or open wires.

* 💬 **Conversational Safety Guidance**
  Users ask questions like “Do I need gloves for this?”
  → **AI (Granite Instruct)** responds with easy-to-understand safety recommendations in **Swahili** or **English**.

* 🔁 **Personalized Nudges and Reminders**
  Linda learns from prior interactions to deliver role-specific tips and motivational nudges for better safety habits.

* 🔒 **Anonymity + Simplicity**
  No apps. No downloads. No data entry. Just WhatsApp.

---

## 🧠 AI Behind the Scenes

* **Granite Vision** (IBM): Computer vision model used to detect unsafe conditions from images.
* **Granite Instruct** (IBM): Natural language model for chat-based interaction, tuned on localized OSH data.

These models adapt over time using real-world data from informal environments in Nairobi.
